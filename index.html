Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to understand Qenrec and how it helps teams start every project with clarity.

Qenrec is a project briefing and client intake platform that helps teams collect, organize, and clarify the information needed before a project begins.

Qenrec is designed for agencies, studios, professional-service teams, and other organizations that regularly start projects with clients and need structured information before execution.

You create or select a project brief, send it to your client, collect their information and files, review what is complete or missing, clarify unanswered questions, and move the project toward ready-to-start.

Yes. Qenrec is designed around reusable templates and customizable questions so teams can build workflows for different project types.

The intended experience is a client-facing brief accessed through a secure project link, allowing the client to complete the required information without entering the agency workspace.

The agency receives the completed project information, can review the submission, identify missing details, request clarifications, and determine whether the project is ready to move into execution.

Qenrec is designed to keep project information, requirements, decisions, and client-provided files organized within the project instead of scattered across different tools. Security, permissions, and privacy controls are part of the product architecture.

Yes. Qenrec helps teams collect the required information, identify missing details, resolve unanswered questions, and confirm that the project has the clarity needed before work begins.
